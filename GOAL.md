### Improvement Board — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the tool with the longest usage tail in the suite — the one teams are
still opening six months later.

#### Why this app can win it

Continuous improvement dies from lack of follow-through, not lack of ideas.
Every retro tool captures actions; almost none survive long enough to show whether
anything changed. Persistence over sprints *is* the product.

#### Signals

1. Consecutive sprints tracked without a gap.
2. Ratio of items resolved to items created — evidence the loop closes.
3. Age of the oldest actively-tracked board.
4. GitHub stars and mentions in continuous-improvement writing.

#### Boundaries

- Not a project tracker or a backlog.
- Not a performance-management instrument; it tracks systems, not people.

*Repo: `agile-toolkit/improvement-board`*

---

## Latitude

Every goal above names an outcome, not a mechanism. Any roadmap that moves a
signal without crossing a boundary is fair game — including approaches nobody
in this category has tried. Invention is expected, not merely permitted.
